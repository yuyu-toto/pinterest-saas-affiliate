Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ClientStack Reviews ("we", "us") operates a Pinterest application used to schedule and publish pins on our own Pinterest business account. This policy explains how that application handles data.

What the application does

Our application connects to the Pinterest API to:

  • Create and publish pins on our own Pinterest account
  • Read our own pin and board data to track performance (clicks, impressions) for our own content

What we do not do

The application does not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user. It is a single-account, personal-use integration — it is not a consumer-facing product and does not authenticate or act on behalf of third-party users.

Data we handle

Pinterest API credentials (access tokens) used by this application are stored privately and are not shared with third parties. No visitor data is collected by this website beyond standard hosting logs generated automatically by GitHub Pages.

Affiliate disclosure

Content linked from our pins may contain affiliate links. We disclose this on every article where it applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact information on our Pinterest business profile.
